Notebook

Decode a VIN & check recalls

Free data from NHTSA. Confirms the year/make/model/engine the seller claims, and lists recall campaigns for that model. Run the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if a specific car's recall was fixed.

Check history (paid / free)

NMVTIS approved providers — cheap title-brand check (salvage, flood, junk) NICB VINCheck — free theft & total-loss check Carfax — full history report AutoCheck — full history report CA BAR smog check history — past smog results by VIN or plate

True cost to buy in Ridgecrest

9.25% is the Ridgecrest city rate. If you live in unincorporated Kern County, use tax is lower — check CDTFA's rate lookup. Tax is charged on where the car is registered, not where you buy it.
